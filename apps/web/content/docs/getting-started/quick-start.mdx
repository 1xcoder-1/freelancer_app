title: Quick start

This guide takes you from an empty account to a sent invoice. It takes about
90 seconds once you are signed in.

## Step 1: Create your account

Go to [/sign-up](/sign-up) and create an account. You land on the dashboard at
[/dashboard](/dashboard). Your workspace is private and scoped to you.

<Callout type="info">
  Signing in later always goes through the same provider you picked at sign
  up. The dashboard and the desktop app share the same account.
</Callout>

## Step 2: Add your first client

1. Open the **Clients** tab.
2. Click **New client** and fill in the name.
3. Add an email and an hourly or project rate if you already know them.

The client now shows up in your roster. Use roster groups to keep sections
like "Design", "Writing" or "Retainers" apart. Clients that go quiet get a
silence signal so you know who to ping.

## Step 3: Start a project

1. Open the **Projects** tab and click **New project**.
2. Pick the client you just created.
3. Choose how you bill: hourly, or milestones with fractions like 30% / 40% /
   30%.
4. Set a budget if the work has a cap.

The project board is a kanban. Move cards as work progresses. Unbilled hours
collect on the project until you invoice them.

## Step 4: Track some time

1. Start the timer from the project page, or press Ctrl+K and type "start".
2. Work. The timer keeps running even if you close the tab or refresh.
3. Stop it when you are done. The entry waits in the project as an unbilled
   hour.

## Step 5: Send your first invoice

1. Open the project and click **Create invoice**.
2. Freelance Book pulls in unbilled hours and finished milestones. Adjust the
   lines if you want.
3. The invoice starts as **Draft**. Click **Send** when it is ready.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Draft**: only you can see it. Edit freely.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Sent**: the client gets a public pay link that works without an account.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Paid** or **Overdue**: mark payments manually, or let automations flag
    overdue invoices during the daily check.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every invoice gets a public pay link you can paste into an email. The client
sees the line items and can pay without creating anything.
