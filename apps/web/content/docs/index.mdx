Freelance Book is a freelancer OS. It replaces the pile of spreadsheets, notes
apps and invoicing tools most solo workers stitch together.

Everything in it maps to how freelance work actually flows:

1. A person becomes a **lead**, then a **client**.
2. A client agrees to a **proposal**, then a **contract**.
3. Work happens in **projects**, tracked with a **timer**.
4. Tracked time and milestones turn into an **invoice**.
5. The invoice gets paid, and your **cash flow** stays honest.

One app keeps all of that connected. You never retype a client name, a rate or
a deliverable.

## Who it is for

- Solo freelancers and contractors who invoice several clients a month.
- Small studios that juggle retainers, milestones and hourly work.
- Anyone who wants fewer tools, not more.

## Run it anywhere

- **Web dashboard** at [/dashboard](/dashboard). Works in any modern browser.
- **Desktop app** for Windows, macOS and Linux. Adds global quick capture with
  Ctrl+Shift+F. See [Desktop and mobile](/docs/apps/desktop-and-mobile).
- **Android app** is coming soon, with offline sync and push notifications.
