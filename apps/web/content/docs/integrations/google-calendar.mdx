The Google Calendar integration does two jobs: it **mirrors** your calendar
into the dashboard calendar, and since bookings now create events, it
**provisions real Google Meet links** for every booked call.

## Connecting

1. Open the **Calendar** page in the dashboard.
2. Click **Connect Google Calendar** and complete Google's consent screen.
3. Events start syncing immediately — manually via **Sync now**, and
   continuously by a background job.

The connection is per workspace and optional; the app works fully without
it.

<Callout type="warning">
Scope upgrade: the integration moved from read-only (`calendar.readonly`)
to event read/write (`calendar.events`) so bookings can create Meet events.
Connections made **before** this change still hold a read-only grant —
disconnect and reconnect once to enable automatic Meet provisioning. Until
you do, bookings keep working with the in-app meeting link as fallback.
</Callout>

## What it does

- **Mirrors your week**: events from your primary calendar are copied into
  the dashboard calendar next to your local events and booked calls, and a
  background job keeps the mirror fresh (events you delete on Google are
  retired in the mirror too).
- **Creates the meeting**: when a client books or reschedules on your
  [booking page](/docs/modules/booking/services), a durable job creates or
  updates an event on your primary calendar with a genuine
  `meet.google.com` link, invites the client, and saves that link on the
  appointment. Reminder emails then contain the live link.
- **Cleans up cancellations**: cancelling an appointment soft-cancels the
  Google event, so attendees get the cancellation automatically.

## What it never does

- It does not read your other calendars or any details beyond event data.
- It never deletes anything on your calendar except events it created for
  bookings.
- Availability on public booking pages is driven by your service hours,
  days off and existing appointments — Google events are **not**
  required for slots to work.

## Data access and revoking

OAuth tokens live on your workspace row only. You can disconnect from the
Calendar page, or revoke the app in your
[Google account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) —
either path de-provisions the feature completely; the mirror simply stops
updating.
