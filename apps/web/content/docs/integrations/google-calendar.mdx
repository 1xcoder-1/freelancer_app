title: Google Calendar
description: Connect Google Calendar to block booked slots and get meeting reminders where you already look.

The Google Calendar integration connects your booking pages and your
automations to the calendar you already use.

## What it does

- **Blocks slots**: events on your calendar make those times unavailable on
  every [booking page](/docs/modules/booking).
- **Writes bookings**: confirmed bookings appear as events on the calendar
  you choose, with attendee and answers in the description.
- **Meeting reminders**: reminders fire as normal calendar notifications,
  next to your other meetings.

## Connecting

1. Open **Settings**, then **Integrations**.
2. Click **Connect Google Calendar** and complete the Google consent screen.
3. Pick which calendar Freelance Book should write to. Your other calendars
   stay read-only for availability checks.

Disconnecting stops all reads and writes. Bookings already on the calendar
stay there.

## Availability rules

Slot generation is the intersection of:

- Your booking page availability window.
- Busy events on your connected calendar.
- The page's minimum notice and per-day limits.

A slot that overlaps any busy event never appears, no matter which calendar
it came from. All-day events block the whole day.

<Callout type="tip">
  If a client reschedules by editing the calendar event directly, the booking
  record in Freelance Book updates on the next sync. Cancelling the event
  cancels the booking and notifies the attendee.
</Callout>

## Data access

The integration requests the smallest scope set that works: read free/busy
and events for the calendar you connect, and write events. It never touches
other calendars or deletes events it did not create.

Tokens are stored per workspace and can be revoked at any time, both from
Settings and from your Google account's security page.
