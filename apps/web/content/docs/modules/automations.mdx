---
title: Automations
description: Daily overdue checks, payment reminders and meeting reminders that run without you.
---

Automations are small jobs that run on your behalf. They exist to remove the
two chores freelancers are worst at: chasing money and remembering meetings.

## Daily overdue check

Once a day, Freelance Book walks every sent invoice and checks it against
the due date:

1. Past due: the invoice flips to **Overdue**.
2. The invoice joins the "Needs attention" list on the dashboard.
3. If payment reminders are on, the next reminder in the sequence goes out.

The check runs on a schedule, not when you happen to open the app, so an
invoice you forgot about still gets flagged on time.

## Payment reminders

Reminders are emails sent through the invoice's pay link, so the client can
settle immediately. Configure per workspace:

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First reminder | 3 days after due date | First nudge |
| Repeat | Every 7 days | Interval between follow-ups |
| Max reminders | 3 | Sequence stops after this |
| Include CC to you | On | You see every reminder |

Reminders are polite by design: a short line, the amount, the due date and
the pay link. Marking an invoice as paid stops the sequence instantly.

## Meeting reminders

When a booking is confirmed, Freelance Book schedules reminders for both
sides:

- A reminder to you ahead of the call, with the booking questions attached.
- An email to the attendee with the time, timezone and a reschedule link.

If you connected Google Calendar, the reminder also lands as a normal
calendar notification. See
[Google Calendar](/docs/integrations/google-calendar).

## Where to configure

Open **Settings** and pick the **Automations** tab. Everything is opt-in
except the overdue status change itself, which is core to keeping the money
view honest.

<Callout type="warning">
  Reminders send from your connected email identity. If you disconnect the
  integration, pending reminders pause rather than fail silently.
</Callout>

## What automations do not do

- They never send invoices for you. Sending stays manual.
- They never edit amounts, dates or statuses other than marking Overdue.
- They never contact a client who opted out of reminders.
