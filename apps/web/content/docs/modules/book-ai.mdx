title: Book AI

Book AI is the assistant built into Freelance Book. It knows your clients,
projects, invoices and time entries, so you never have to paste context into
a chat window.

## Proposal drafting

The main use: turning scattered inputs into a first-draft proposal.

Start a proposal, click **Draft with Book AI**, and give it the raw
material:

- The client and what they asked for.
- A booking, intake form or email text.
- A rough budget or a rate.

Book AI drafts sections from that: understanding, scope, deliverables,
timeline and a pricing table. The pricing table uses the client's real rate
from the CRM and can carry milestone splits from a project template.

You edit from there. The draft lands in the normal proposal editor, with
[revisions](/docs/modules/proposals-and-contracts) tracking every published
version.

## Business Q&A

Ask questions in plain language. The assistant answers from your workspace
records and shows what it used:

- "How much did I bill Acme this quarter?"
- "Which client has the highest effective hourly rate?"
- "What is unbilled on the Northwind project right now?"
- "Did I reply to Dana from Bridgeport recently?"

Answers cite the records they came from, with links. If a question cannot be
answered from your data, it says so instead of guessing.

<Callout type="info">
  Book AI only reads data inside your workspace. It cannot see other
  workspaces, other users' data, or anything outside Freelance Book.
</Callout>

## Good inputs, good drafts

The draft is only as good as the context:

1. Keep client rates and billing details current in the CRM.
2. Attach intake form answers to the lead before drafting.
3. Mention constraints in the prompt: deadline, budget ceiling, what is out
   of scope.

## Where it fits

- **Proposals**: draft, then refine and send for e-sign.
- **Command menu**: Ctrl+K and start typing a question to open Book AI
  prefilled.
- **Report Card**: the same data feeds the on-time delivery, payment
  reliability and effective rate metrics, no AI involved.
