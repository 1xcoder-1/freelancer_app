---
title: Booking
description: Public self-serve booking pages that create leads and connect to your calendar.
---

The Booking module gives every freelancer a public page where prospects pick
a time and book a call. It removes the "does Tuesday at 3 work for you?"
email thread.

## How it works

1. Create a booking page and give it a name, like "Intro call".
2. Define what you offer: a duration, a description and optional questions.
3. Set your availability: which days, which hours, and your timezone.
4. Share the page URL anywhere: email signature, portfolio, social profile.

Visitors pick a free slot, answer your questions and confirm. The booking
lands in your dashboard and on your calendar.

## Availability

Availability rules are per page, so you can offer different windows for
different services:

- Working hours with a buffer between calls.
- Day-of-week limits, for example no Mondays.
- Minimum notice, for example 12 hours ahead.
- A maximum of bookings per day.

Existing calendar events block slots automatically if you connect Google
Calendar. See [Google Calendar](/docs/integrations/google-calendar).

## Where bookings land

A confirmed booking creates or updates a **lead** in the Clients module:

- New visitor: a new lead with the answers from your booking questions.
- Existing client: the booking is attached to their record.

From there, the normal pipeline applies: contact them, qualify them, send a
proposal.

<Callout type="tip">
  Add one required question like "What are you trying to get done?" It makes
  the first call twice as useful, and the answers become Book AI context for
  drafting the proposal.
</Callout>

## Public page behavior

- Booking pages work without an account and on mobile.
- Cancel and reschedule links are included in the confirmation.
- Timezones are handled for the visitor, so nobody books "your" 3 AM by
  accident.
- You can pause a page at any time; the URL then shows a plain
  "not accepting bookings" note.
