The **Calls** page (`/dashboard/booking`) is your call pipeline. Four stat
cards on top show the next call and how long until it, upcoming calls (plus
today and the next 7 days), what needs you (to confirm, awaiting payment),
and recorded no-shows. Everything else is one card per appointment, newest
bookings first.

## Status machine

| Status | Meaning | Who moves it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pending | Booked; paid calls wait for the invoice to be settled | Created by booking |
| confirmed | The call is on | Payment lands (paid) or you confirm (free) |
| completed | The call happened — the person is promoted to a Client record | You |
| cancelled | Dead slot, freed for rebooking | You or the client |
| no-show | Client missed it — counts as a strike against their email | You |

## Client self-service

Every appointment gets its own scoped token and the client gets a link
(`/reschedule/{token}`) where they can, without emailing you:

- view their appointment and its status,
- reschedule to another server-validated slot — up to **3 times**, and the
  overlap check excludes their own current slot,
- cancel.

Reschedules re-fire the reminder workflow, so the client always gets the
link for the *new* time.

## Google Meet + calendar events

With [Google Calendar connected](/docs/integrations/google-calendar), a real
Google Calendar event with a **live Meet link** is created for every new or
rescheduled booking, and cancelled when the appointment is cancelled. The
reminder emails re-read the appointment at send time, so they always contain
the current link.

## Money

- Paid service: booking creates an invoice automatically; the appointment
  confirms only when that invoice is marked **paid** by a real payment.
- Free service: confirmed instantly, `payment_status = free`.

## Reminders

Two durable jobs per booking: an email 24 hours before the call and a
short-notice nudge 1 hour before — both with the meeting link.

<Callout type="info">
Booked calls also show up on the dashboard calendar, merged with your local
events and mirrored Google events — one view for the whole week. See
[Google Calendar](/docs/integrations/google-calendar).
</Callout>
