---
title: Days off
description: Block whole dates per service — holidays, travel, "away this week" — so no slot is ever offered on them.
---

**Days off** block entire dates on one service. Open the *Days off* row on a
service card (or the Days off card on its edit page), pick a date, and every
slot on that day disappears from the public page.

## Rules

- Days off are per day, per service — close your strategy session for a
  conference without closing your intro calls.
- They only remove future slots; appointments already booked on that day are
  **not** cancelled. Reach out to those clients yourself (the
  [Calls](/docs/modules/booking/calls) page lists them).
- Removing a day off immediately makes its slots bookable again, subject
  to your normal hours and minimum notice.

## When to use it

| Situation | Better tool |
| --- | --- |
| One-off day off | Add it under Days off |
| Recurring — every Friday | Just uncheck Friday in the service's weekday pills |
| One hour of quiet | A buffer between calls on the service |
| Fully paused | Toggle the service inactive |
