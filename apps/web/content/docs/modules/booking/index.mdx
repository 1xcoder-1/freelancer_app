title: Booking
description: Public self-serve booking pages that turn visitors into booked calls — services, calls and days off, each on its own page.
---

Booking sits right after Home in the dashboard sidebar because it is the top
of your funnel: prospects pick a time on a public page, and the call lands in
your calendar with a real Google Meet link attached. No "does Tuesday at 3
work?" email thread.

It splits into three pages, documented one per page:

| Page | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| [Calls](/docs/modules/booking/calls) | Every appointment clients made — status, reschedules, payment |
| [Services](/docs/modules/booking/services) | Your bookable consultations — duration, price, opening hours |
| [Days off](/docs/modules/booking/days-off) | Dates you are away, so nobody can book them |

`/dashboard/booking` is the Calls page; `/dashboard/booking/services` is
Services. Days off are not a page of their own — they belong to one service,
so they open inside each service card and on its edit page.

## How a booking happens

1. A visitor opens your public page (`/booking/{token}` — share it in an email
   signature, your [Link Page](/docs/modules/report-card/link-page) or social
   profile).
2. They see only slots that are genuinely open: weekdays and hours you set,
   minus days off, minus anything already booked, minus your minimum
   notice.
3. They enter name, email and optional notes and confirm.
4. The appointment appears on **Calls**; the client gets a 24-hour
   reminder and a 1-hour nudge with the meeting link.

## Every rule is enforced server-side

Slot lists are only a hint — the booking request itself re-validates
everything inside one transaction, so a stale tab or a bot can never
double-book, book a past time, or ignore your days off:

- Weekday window + daily opening hours, per service.
- Minimum lead time and a maximum advance window.
- A buffer you can require around each call.
- Overlap against live appointment data at insert time.

## Paid consultations

A service with a price is not "paid because a price exists". Booking one
creates an invoice, and the appointment only flips to confirmed when that
invoice actually reaches **paid** (handled by a durable workflow). Free
services confirm immediately.

## No-show protection

Each person gets a strike counter per workspace. Once their no-shows reach
the service's limit, free slots are refused for them — their next booking
must be a paid one, so prepayment is enforced where it matters.

<Callout type="tip">
Paid consultations link an intake form, so you get answers before the call —
and those answers make excellent
[Book AI](/docs/modules/proposals/book-ai) context afterwards.
</Callout>
