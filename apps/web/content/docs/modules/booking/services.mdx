title: Services

A **service** (consultation) is one bookable offering: "Intro call — 30
minutes, free" or "Strategy session — 60 minutes, paid". Each one has its own
public page, its own availability window and its own rules.

## Creating one

**Services → New service** (`/dashboard/booking/services`) opens a full-page
editor (same layout as client pages, not a popup), with a live preview of your
public page in the right column. A brand-new workspace has no services at
all — you create the first one; nothing is pre-filled, and nothing you delete
comes back.

| Field | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| Title, description | What the visitor sees first |
| Duration | Slot length in minutes |
| Price | `0` = free (instant confirm); above 0 = invoice + paid-then-confirmed |
| Weekdays | Which days are bookable — Monday to Sunday pills |
| Opening hours | Daily start/end in your timezone |
| Minimum notice | How far ahead a client must book (e.g. 2 hours) |
| Maximum advance | How far into the future slots appear (default 60 days) |
| Buffer | Quiet minutes kept around each call |
| Intake form | Optional — attached to every booking of this service |
| No-show limit | Strikes allowed before free slots are refused |

## On each card

Every service card carries the whole life cycle of that service, so there is
no fourth screen to look for:

- **Copy link** — the public `/booking/{token}` URL, ready to paste.
- **Open** (external-link icon) — the public page itself, in a new tab.
- **Edit** — the same full-page editor, prefilled, with a
  [Days off](/docs/modules/booking/days-off) card underneath.
- **Pause / resume** — stops taking bookings without losing the URL.
- **Delete** — gone for good, and it stays gone.
- **Days off** — expands to the per-service away dates.

## Public page behavior

- Inactive services stop taking bookings immediately; the URL then shows a
  plain "not accepting bookings" note.
- Slots are generated in 30-day horizons at most, within your max-advance
  window.
- Editing availability never invalidates appointments already taken — it only
  changes which future slots appear.

<Callout type="tip">
Run two services side by side: a free 20-minute filter call and a paid 60-minute
deep dive. The free one carries the no-show limit, the paid one is prepaid —
together they keep your calendar full and your time cheap to give away.
</Callout>
