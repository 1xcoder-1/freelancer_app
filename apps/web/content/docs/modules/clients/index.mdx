The Clients module is the roster your whole workspace hangs off. Every
project, invoice, proposal and booking link points back to a client here.

## The roster

Each client card shows the essentials: contact details, active projects, open
invoice total and the last time you heard from them. Open a client to see
their full history in one place.

Organize the roster with **groups**. A group is just a labeled section, like
"Design clients", "Retainers" or "Past work". Groups keep a long roster
readable and make the sidebar, filters and exports follow the same shape.

## VIP and silence signals

Two lightweight signals keep attention where it matters:

- **VIP**: star a client to pin them to the top of the roster. VIP clients
  get their own accent in lists and are sorted first in the command menu.
- **Silence signal**: if a client has not been contacted within the window
  you set, their card shows a quiet warning. Useful for spotting
  relationships that are cooling off before they end.

## Intake forms and leads

Two tabs sit beside the roster:

- **Intake Forms** — shareable questionnaires that collect scope, budget
  and deadlines before the first call. See
  [Intake Forms](/docs/modules/clients/intake).
- **Leads Pipeline** — everyone interested but not paying yet, with stages,
  follow-up signals and touchpoint history. See
  [Leads Pipeline](/docs/modules/clients/leads).

<Callout type="tip">
  Connect the [Booking](/docs/modules/booking) module to let prospects book a
  call from a public page. Bookings create or enrich a lead automatically.
</Callout>

## Where client data is used

- **Projects**: every project belongs to a client.
- **Invoices**: invoice lines and pay links use the client's billing details.
- **Proposals** and **Contracts**: drafts copy the client's name, rate and scope.
- **Book AI**: answers about a client read from this module first.
