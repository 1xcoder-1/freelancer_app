title: Intake Forms

Intake forms collect structured info from a person before you ever talk
to them. You build the form once, share its link, and the answers arrive
attached to a lead instead of lost in an email thread.

## Building a form

Find the builder under **Clients → Intake Forms** (or `/dashboard/forms`).
The panel keeps count of your **active flows**, their **categories** and
total **responses**, so you can see at a glance which questionnaires are
working.

A flow is a named questionnaire: add the questions you actually need —
scope, budget, deadline, references — and group forms with categories if
you run more than one kind of intake.

## Sharing

- Forms are shareable by URL. The respondent needs no account.
- A shareable public link renders the questionnaire on a clean page.

## Where answers go

Every submission lands in the **Leads** tab with the form fields filled,
and the sender becomes a lead automatically:

- The answers travel with the lead into their detail view.
- Convert a lead into a client with one click — the intake answers move
  with them, so nothing gets retyped.
- [Book AI](/docs/modules/proposals/book-ai) can read the answers when drafting a
  proposal for that person.

<Callout type="tip">
Pair an intake form with the [Booking](/docs/modules/booking) module:
let prospects book a call from a public page and the booking creates or
enriches the matching lead.
</Callout>
