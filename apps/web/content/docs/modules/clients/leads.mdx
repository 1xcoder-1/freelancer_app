The Leads module tracks people who are interested but not paying yet. It
lives next to your roster under **Clients → Leads Pipeline**, so a lead and
a client are always one conversion apart — never two systems.

## The pipeline

Every lead carries a stage so the state of your sales is visible at a
glance:

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| New | Arrived, not yet reviewed |
| Contacted | You reached out |
| Proposal | A proposal is out |
| Talking price | Negotiating the number |
| Won | Converted to a client |
| Lost | Parked, with a note on why |

The board header does the arithmetic for you: **pipeline value**, **total
leads**, **ongoing leads** and **follow-ups due**, computed from the cards
below.

## Needs action

The default view is **Needs action** — only the leads that want something
from you right now: a reply due, a follow-up overdue, or a stage that has
gone quiet. Flip to **All leads** when you want the whole pipeline, and
page through cards twenty at a time.

## Touchpoints and silence

Every interaction — a call, an email, a meeting — is logged on the lead as
a **touchpoint**. From that history the card computes how long the person
has been silent, so "X days silent" is a fact on the card, not a memory in
your head.

- Add a touchpoint with a direction (in or out) and a note.
- The silence counter resets on every new interaction.
- Follow-ups surface in **Needs action** when the silence passes your
  window.

## One person, one record

If the same person shows up twice — say, they filled an intake form and
later booked a call — the workspace merges them into a single person
record instead of duplicating the card. Bookings from your
[public booking page](/docs/modules/booking) create or enrich the matching
lead automatically.

## Converting

When a lead wins, convert them to a client with one click. The contact
details, touchpoint history and intake answers move with them, and the
lead's archive entry stays so you can see where the client came from.

<Callout type="tip">
Intake forms feed this module: every submission lands as a new lead with
the form fields pre-filled. See [Clients Roster](/docs/modules/clients).
</Callout>
