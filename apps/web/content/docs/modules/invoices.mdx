The money module covers the invoice lifecycle, the expenses you pay to work,
and the cash flow picture both produce together.

## Invoice lifecycle

Every invoice moves through one simple pipeline:

| Status | Who sees it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | Only you | Edit lines, rates and notes freely |
| Sent | Client via pay link | Locked lines, due date starts ticking |
| Paid | Archive | Marked with the date paid |
| Overdue | Client | Flagged after the due date passes |

You can move an invoice backward to Draft before it is paid, for example to
fix a wrong line. Every status change is recorded with a timestamp.

## Public pay links

Each invoice gets a **public pay link**. Paste it into an email or a chat and
the client opens a clean page with the line items, totals and your payment
details. No account, no portal, no password.

- The link is unlisted and works for anyone who has it.
- You can regenerate the link to invalidate the old one.
- The page shows the invoice status, so a paid invoice reads as paid.

## The overdue flow

The [Automations](/docs/modules/automations) module runs a daily overdue
check. When an invoice passes its due date:

1. The invoice flips to **Overdue**.
2. The dashboard shows it in the "Needs attention" list.
3. A reminder email goes out if you enabled payment reminders.

Reminders are polite and bounded: you choose the interval and the maximum
number of follow-ups per invoice.

## Expenses

Log expenses per client or per project, with a date, an amount and a
category. Expenses you plan to recharge can be attached to the next invoice
for that project as line items.

The Expenses view supports a monthly total per category, so tax time does
not start with a shoebox of receipts.

## Cash flow

The cash flow view puts invoices and expenses on one timeline:

- **In**: sent and paid invoices, by expected and actual date.
- **Out**: expenses, by date.
- **Net**: what is actually left.

Future months use due dates and recurring expenses as estimates, so you can
see a slow month coming weeks ahead.

<Callout type="tip">
  The Report Card module computes your effective hourly rate from paid

  worth the hours.
</Callout>
