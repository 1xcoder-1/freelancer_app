The Money module — **Money** in the sidebar — tracks what goes out, so
the cashflow picture stays honest against what
[invoices](/docs/modules/projects/invoices) bring in.

## Recording an expense

An expense is a description, an amount and a category. Categories keep
spending readable at tax time and in summaries:

- Software & Subscriptions
- Hardware & Equipment
- Internet & Utilities
- Office & Coworking
- Advertising & Marketing
- General

## One-time vs recurring

Every expense is either **one-time** or **recurring monthly**:

- **Make Recurring** marks a cost that repeats — subscriptions, rent,
  retainers. Recurring expenses count toward your **monthly run rate**,
  the number you need to clear before a month is actually profitable.
- **Make One-Time** is for purchases that should not pollute the run
  rate — a laptop, a one-off license.

Change your mind later: flip an expense between the two states from its
card without deleting and re-entering it.

## Keeping cashflow honest

The run rate from recurring expenses flows into the cashflow view
alongside invoice totals, so "how much do I need to bill this month" is a
lookup, not a spreadsheet session.

<Callout type="tip">
Public pay links on invoices ([Invoices & Billing](/docs/modules/projects/invoices))
are the fastest way to close the gap between money owed and money in.
</Callout>
