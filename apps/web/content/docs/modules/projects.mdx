Projects are where work happens. A project belongs to one client and carries
its own billing model, budget and board.

## Billing models

When you create a project you pick how it gets billed:

- **Hourly**: you track time, and unbilled hours accumulate until you invoice
  them. The project remembers your rate, with optional overrides per task.
- **Milestones**: you split the project into fractions, for example 30% /
  40% / 30%. Each milestone can be marked done and invoiced on its own, so
  cash arrives during the project instead of after it.

Milestone fractions are editable at any time. When the fractions no longer
sum to 100%, the project shows a small warning instead of silently rounding.

## Budgets

Set a budget in hours, in money, or both. The project header shows a burn
bar as tracked time and approved expenses stack up.

- At 80% of budget you get a soft warning on the project and in the
  dashboard.
- Past 100% the bar turns red and new time entries get flagged as
  over-budget.

## The board

Every project has a kanban board. Columns are simple: To do, In progress,
Review and Done. Cards can hold a description, a time estimate and links to
invoices they belong to.

You can move cards freely. Nothing in the board changes money by itself;
invoicing stays an explicit step in the [Invoices](/docs/modules/invoices)
module.

## Unbilled hours

Time entries that have not been attached to an invoice are listed on the
project as **unbilled hours**. When you create an invoice for the project,
Freelance Book offers those hours as draft line items.

<Callout type="info">
  Entries stay unbilled until an invoice containing them is sent. Editing a
  draft invoice never locks the underlying time entries.
</Callout>

## Project page at a glance

- Burn bar against budget.
- Milestone list with fractions and status.
- Unbilled hours total.
- Recent time entries.
- Related invoices and proposals.
