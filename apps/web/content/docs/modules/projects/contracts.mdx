Contracts are the paperwork behind a "yes" — the agreement a client signs
before work starts. They live under **Projects → Contracts** in the
sidebar, next to the projects they govern.

## Same editor, same pipeline

Contracts share their editor, share links and status pipeline with
[Proposals](/docs/modules/proposals): section blocks, pricing tables that
pull from client and project data, and the three states — **Sent**,
**Viewed**, **Executed**.

## A signed contract is never edited

The revision mechanism matters most here. A contract that has been signed
is locked; to change the deal you draft a **new revision** and it must be
signed again.

- Every revision has a number, a date and a diff from the previous one.
- Share links point at one specific revision, so what the client signed
  stays stable and quotable later.
- The executed revision is kept as a timestamped copy — that is your
  record of the deal as agreed.

## E-sign

Send the contract with a **share link**. The client opens it in a browser
and signs by typing their legal name. No account needed.

| Setting | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expiry | 30 days | Link stops accepting signatures after this |
| Signer name | Free text | Stored with the signature |
| Notifications | On | You get an email on view and on sign |

<Callout type="info">
E-sign here is a simple typed signature with an audit trail (who, when,
what was signed). For jurisdictions that require certified digital
signatures, download the executed copy and run it through your local tool.
</Callout>
