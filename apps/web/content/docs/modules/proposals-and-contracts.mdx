---
title: Proposals and contracts
description: Draft proposals, share e-sign links with expiry, keep versioned revisions and track Sent, Viewed and Executed.
---

Proposals and contracts turn a "yes" into signed paperwork. Both share the
same editor, the same share links and the same status pipeline.

## Drafting

A proposal starts blank or from Book AI. You write sections with simple
blocks: headings, paragraphs, lists and pricing tables.

Pricing tables understand the rest of the app:

- Pull a rate from the client record.
- Pull milestone splits from a project.
- Total themselves as you edit.

## Revisions

Every published version of a proposal is kept as a **revision**. When you
change a proposal that a client has already seen, you publish a new revision
instead of editing history.

- Each revision has a number, a date and a full diff from the previous one.
- Share links point at one specific revision, so "the PDF you sent" stays
  stable.
- Contracts use the same mechanism: a signed contract is never edited, a new
  revision is drafted and must be signed again.

## Share links and e-sign

Send a proposal or contract with a **share link**. The client opens it in a
browser, reads it and signs by typing their legal name. No account needed.

| Setting | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expiry | 30 days | Link stops accepting signatures after this |
| Signer name | Free text | Stored with the signature |
| Notifications | On | You get an email on view and on sign |

You can set a shorter expiry for time-sensitive quotes. An expired link shows
a friendly page, and you can re-issue a fresh link from the proposal page.

## Status pipeline

Proposals and contracts both track three states:

1. **Sent**: the link exists and works. Nothing observed yet.
2. **Viewed**: the recipient opened the link. You see when, and from what
   device class.
3. **Executed**: signed. The document is locked and a timestamped copy is
   kept.

A proposal that is executed can be converted into a project in one click.
Scope sections carry over as the project description and the pricing table
becomes milestone fractions.

<Callout type="info">
  E-sign here is a simple typed signature with an audit trail (who, when,
  what was signed). For jurisdictions that require certified digital
  signatures, download the executed copy and run it through your local tool.
</Callout>
