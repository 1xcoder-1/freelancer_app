The Report Card is your public face inside the book — a shareable profile
page that you compose from the work you already track. Find it in the
sidebar under **Profile → Report Card**, with **[Link Page](/docs/modules/report-card/link-page)**
as the second Profile sub-page.

## What it is

Most freelancers need the same page five times a year: for a client who
asks "who are you?", for a conference bio, for the footer of a proposal.
The Report Card is that page, built once, kept current from your real
data, and published at a link you can paste anywhere.

## Tabs

The page has four tabs, each answering a different question:

| Tab | Answers |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Who you are — bio, focus areas, companies, writing and the quote you want to be known by |
| **Inspiration** | What shapes your taste — the people, studios and writing you draw from |
| **Projects** | What you have made — a grid of work entries you compose and edit directly |
| **Sponsor** | How to support you — for people who want to back your work directly |

Compose each tab from sections: company grids, writing lists, item lists,
a quote block and a footer — arranged in the order that tells your story.

## Templates and typography

The settings popover carries a **Template** picker — Classic (the original,
follows your app theme), Editorial, Midnight and Warm Amber — each one
restyling the card and leading with the section that suits it. Within a
template you still pick the voice: Schibsted Grotesk, Inter or Geist. See
[Templates](/docs/modules/report-card/templates).

## Profile Dashboard

Below the card preview, the page carries a **Profile Dashboard**: a shortcut
to edit your Link Page and a compact live view of its traffic — real
analytics from every recorded view and click on your public pages.

## Sync and speed

Edits save to the database as you work — <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>
forces a save, and the page stays in sync across devices: open it on your
phone while editing on your desktop and the change is already there.
Frequently used data is cached locally, so the page loads fast even on a
weak connection.

## Sharing

The published page works without an account on the visitor's side. Share
the link in an email signature, a proposal footer, or your Link Page.

<Callout type="tip">
The page also shows your working rhythm — streaks, active days and an
activity heatmap are computed automatically from your
[time tracking](/docs/modules/time-tracking) and project activity, so the
public profile stays honest without extra effort.
</Callout>
