The **Link Page** is the second Profile sub-page in the sidebar
(**Profile → Link Page**). You enter your links once; we render them as a
fast, styled page at `yoursite/p/your-slug` — and track every view and
click against it.

## The builder

One page, three parts:

1. **Identity** — slug (shown with the `/p/` prefix), display name,
   headline, bio and avatar initials.
2. **Template picker** — six hand-built layouts, each shown as a live
   miniature preview you can click before choosing:

   | Template | Feel |
   | --- | --- |
   | Classic Dark | Timeless Linktree-style dark page, pill buttons |
   | Editorial Minimal | Quiet light layout, serif type, underline links |
   | Aurora Gradient | Vivid gradient backdrop, frosted glass cards |
   | Studio Bold | Black canvas, oversized type, numbered rows |
   | Warm Cards | Soft two-column cards with descriptions |
   | Terminal Neon | Monospace glow for developers and designers |

3. **Links** — label, URL (auto-prefixed with `https://`), optional emoji
   icon and description, visible/hidden toggle, reorder and delete.

A sticky live preview mirrors the page as you edit. **Publish** makes the
slug publicly reachable; **Unpublish** turns it off without losing any
data. The pill in the header tells you whether you are live or on a draft.

## Stats that are real

The stats panel at the bottom of the builder — and a compact copy on your
Report Card page — reads the actual event stream:

- **Views / clicks / CTR** for the last 7, 30 or 90 days.
- A day-by-day area chart of both series.
- **Top links** ranked by clicks.
- **Referrers** — where visitors came from.

Every view and click is recorded server-side when someone opens
`/p/your-slug`, so the numbers come from real traffic, not page
estimates.

<Callout type="tip">
Put the Link Page URL in your social bios — it is the one link you can
rearrange at any time without touching any other platform. Hidden links
keep their stats running, so you can decide later whether to show them
again.
</Callout>
