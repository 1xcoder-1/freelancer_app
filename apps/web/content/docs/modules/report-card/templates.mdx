title: Templates
description: Four looks for your public profile — Classic, Editorial, Midnight and Warm Amber — each restyling the card and reordering the Home sections.
---

The settings popover on the **Report Card** editor has a **Template** grid
above the font picker. The choice saves with your card settings, so the
published page renders the same way for every visitor.

| Template | Look | Home leads with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Classic** | The original — follows your app theme | Bio → focus areas → companies → work → stats → quote |
| **Editorial** | Warm paper tones, magazine feel | The quote, then bio |
| **Midnight** | Deep navy dashboard feel | Stats up front |
| **Warm Amber** | Sepia dark canvas | Work first, after a short bio |

## What a template changes

- **Colors**: background, surface and text tones of the card itself — the
  rest of your dashboard is untouched.
- **Section order on Home**: the same blocks, arranged to open with what the
  template is best at. A stats-led midnight card reads like a dashboard; an
  editorial paper card reads like a magazine intro.

## What it doesn't change

- Your content — every section keeps its data across templates.
- Fonts — pick Schibsted Grotesk, Inter or Geist independently of the
  template.
- The other tabs (Inspiration, Projects, Sponsor).

<Callout type="tip">
Choosing the slug of your [Link Page](/docs/modules/report-card/link-page)
and the template of your profile are the two decisions visitors actually
remember. Keep them consistent: Midnight profile + Terminal Neon link page
reads as one brand.
</Callout>
