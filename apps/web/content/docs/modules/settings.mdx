title: Settings
description: Business identity, invoicing defaults, data export and cloud storage — the workspace-level knobs in one place.
---

Settings holds the workspace-level knobs — things you set once and rarely
touch again. Open **Settings** in the sidebar.

## Business

Your business **name** and default **hourly rate**. These feed the rest of
the app: proposals pull the rate into pricing tables, invoices and the
public profile use the business name.

## Invoicing

Defaults the invoice builder starts from, so you are not re-choosing them
every time:

- **Invoice prefix** for your numbering scheme.
- Default due terms such as **Due on Receipt** or a fixed window.

## Data

Your data stays yours. From here you can **export your data** and download
a **backup** — a CSV export for records and a full backup file you can
keep anywhere. The export is the same data the app uses, not a crippled
copy.

## Cloud

Connected cloud storage for files your workspace keeps — assets, attachments
and media — handled through the storage integration rather than on your
device.
